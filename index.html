How to read the numbers. Market caps are public-market values in USD, pulled from live financial data sources in the first week of July 2026 — they drift daily and cross-listed/ADR figures can vary ~5–20% by source and moment, so treat these as directional, not exact-to-the-dollar. "Share of tier" is a company's cap as a % of that tier's combined priced total — a valuation-weight measure, not a product or revenue market-share figure. Private marks companies confirmed not to be independently publicly traded (family-, foundation-, PE-owned, or a wholly-owned subsidiary of a listed parent).
