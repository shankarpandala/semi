DATA SCOPE — — companies across the full sand-to-system chain, — of them listed. A company is included when its main business, or a named division with outside customers, sits in one of the ten tiers. Each company appears once, in the tier of its main business, and each tier is split into groups by what its companies make. Out of scope: solar, display panels, passive components, printed circuit boards other than IC substrates, component distributors and LED lighting. Tier 10 covers hyperscalers, device and server brands and large contract manufacturers, not every buyer of chips.
MARKET DATA — your browser fetches prices and company market caps from TradingView whenever you open this page or refresh market data. Your browser converts prices and caps to USD using fetched exchange rates. Tier totals, rankings, and weights use only the market caps received in that request; unavailable companies are excluded. Quotes may be delayed or reflect the last market close. “Fetched” is the retrieval time, not the trading time. Private companies and subsidiaries have no standalone public valuation, and companies listed only on the Beijing Stock Exchange are shown without one because the data provider does not carry that exchange.
